Why Memory Matters

Without shared operational memory, every shift may begin without knowing what happened before. OpsRelay aims to connect previous reports, actions, and unresolved issues so teams can continue from where the previous shift stopped.

Demo notice: Timeline events are illustrative sample records. They are not actual memories retrieved from Hindsight. Real memory retrieval will be connected through the backend.
